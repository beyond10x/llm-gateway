A section heading is 0.95rem of a 14 px root, 13.3 px bold, while table cells and definitions are
14 px (the key-data floor of UXF-004) and the page title is 15.4 px. In the contact sheets the
headings "Overview", "In flight", "Routes" differ from the rows under them by weight only. The R-05
heuristic review judged this "not a defect" because the cards are consistent and calm; the rating
disagrees: a reader who scans for a section has only weight and the count chip to find it, and
table cells carry more size than the heading above them. Sessions: all four, both themes, every
viewport. Cost S. One line: set the heading above the cell size (for example 1.1rem) and add a
break test that every `h2` is larger than the cells under it.

Closed in R-07. The heading size of the document is 1.1rem (15.4 px) and a limit card title 1rem
(14 px), so a section heading is larger than the table cells under it. The regression test reads
every h2 and h3 in busy and errors at 1366x768, the text inside them included and the count badge
and muted note left out (secondary text, floored at 12 px), and compares each h2 with the cells of
its section. The key-data gate holds no known failure for UXF-021 any more.
