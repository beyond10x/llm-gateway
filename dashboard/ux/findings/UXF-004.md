Heuristic 15 asks for key data at 14 px or more. The smallest key-data font is 13 px on the idle
page (the connection status) and 10.4 px with data (the vLLM badge, the upstream model under a route,
the pod name under a pod id): a deficit of 1 and 3.6 px. The ratchet holds the deficit from growing.

Closed in R-03: the root font is 14 px, so table cells, definitions, the connection status, status
pills, flags and monospace values are 14 px; tile second lines (rates, spend, resets) are 14 px; and
secondary text (second lines under a name, badges, column heads, captions, tile and stat labels) is
12.25 px, up from 10.4. The Routes column heads wrap so that all 13 columns still fit at 1280x720.
key_font.deficit_px as the harness measures it counts second lines and badges inside table cells as
key data, so it goes from 3.6 to 1.75, not to 0.

Closed in R-04: the regression test and the metric now read one definition of key data,
`keyDataProbe` in `dashboard/e2e/probe.ts`. Key data is the text of table body cells (token counts,
rates, statuses, money, route names), definitions, tile values and their second lines, and the
connection status; a second line under a name (`.small`), a badge, a caption, a column head and a
tile or stat label are secondary text, also inside a key-data cell. Under it `key_font.deficit_px`
is 0 in every scenario (1.75 before, which counted the 12.25 px badges and second lines as key
data) and the new `secondary_font.deficit_px` is 0 against its 12 px floor. The assertion is now a
gate on every gating replay, every state and every action, in both themes, instead of a replay of
its own.
