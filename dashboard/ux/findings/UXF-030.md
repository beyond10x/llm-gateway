The R-07 scroll cue (UXF-017) is a paragraph that exists only while its table is wider than its
box. When a state makes a table scroll, the line is inserted above the table and pushes everything
below it down. Measured with the R-06 and the R-07 page under the same harness at 390x844:
`layout_shift.per_state_max` errors 0 to 0.016, offline 0.012 to 0.017. The ratchet takes the worst
of the gating viewports, where no table scrolls, so it does not see this. Scenarios: errors and
offline at 390x844. Cost S: keep the cue's line in the layout whenever the region can scroll, or
place the cue over the table's edge.
