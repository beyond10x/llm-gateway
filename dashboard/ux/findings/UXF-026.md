In busy the line reads "Failed calls in the last 5 min: claude-sonnet 1 of 3". The link lands on
Routes, which reads claude-sonnet 160 calls, 2 errors and qwen-coder 131 calls, 1 error: no 5 minute
window, a second red route the line did not name, and a count (2) unlike the line's (1). The
failed calls themselves, with their status, time and attempts, are in Recent calls, further down.
"1 of 3" does not say it counts calls, and the line has no word for "claude-sonnet 4 of 4" beyond
the route name. A reader cannot check the line against the screen it opens. Heuristics 5 and 10.
Sessions: busy, errors. Cost S. One line: link to Recent calls (or word the line as "1 of 3 calls
failed" and say the table counts since start).

Closed in R-07. The line and a new Routes column, "Failed, last 5 min", read one count of the
last 5 minutes per route (`recentByRoute`), with the gateway's definition of an error (an outcome
other than ok or a status of 400 or more, not a call the client left). The line names every route
that failed in the window, says "failed", and gives the status and kind of error of its failed
calls; Calls and Errors say on hover that they count since the gateway started. In busy the line
reads "Failed calls in the last 5 min: claude-sonnet 1 of 3 failed, 429 rate limited" beside
Routes' "1 of 3"; in errors, at its last state, "claude-sonnet 4 of 4 failed, 429 rate limited;
qwen-coder 1 of 1 failed, 504 timed out". The line still links to Routes. The regression test
compares the line with the Routes rows at every state of busy and errors.
