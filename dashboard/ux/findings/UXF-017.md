At 390x844 the In flight table shows Route and a model cut at "Qwen/Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B-Ins", and the
State, Wire, Elapsed and Session columns are out of view; Routes shows Route, Calls and Errors; the
Sessions header is cut at "Cancel" at the region edge. The first column of the three call tables is
the one a person needs least; State is what In flight exists for. Nothing says the table scrolls
sideways (the hairline scrollbar of a desktop browser aside), so a reader on a phone takes the cut
table for the whole table. Routes also has a blank band above its header row. UXF-007 closed this at
the gating viewports only; 390 is measured, not gated. Heuristic 3. Sessions: all four, both themes.
One line: show an edge cue on a scrolling table, or at narrow widths put the key column (State) first.

Closed in R-07. A table region measures itself on mount, on resize and on scroll. While columns
are hidden to its right, its right edge fades into the surface colour and the line above it reads
"scroll for more →", which is also the region's accessible description; while columns are hidden
to its left, the left edge fades. Scrolled to its end, the words, the description and the right
fade are gone; the line itself stays while the table can scroll, so scrolling sideways moves
nothing, and at the gating viewports, where no table scrolls, there is no line. The regression
test finds every element that scrolls sideways at 390x844 in busy and errors (in busy the Pods,
In flight, Routes, Sessions and Recent calls tables), checks the cue, scrolls each to its end and
checks it again, and checks that no region carries a cue at 1366x768.
