fill the row and "Provider balance" sits alone on a second row (`contact-1280x720-dark.png`); at
1366x768 it fits. It costs one tile row of the first screen (see UXF-019) and the figure that costs
money is the one set apart. The R-05 heuristic review saw it in a scratch shot and did not file it.
Sessions: busy, errors, token-flow (with a provider), 1280x720. Cost S. One line: keep the balance
on the Overview row (a narrower minimum or a fixed five-column row at desktop widths) and gate that
the tiles of Overview share one row at both gating viewports.

Closed in R-07. Cache hit rate and Throughput each span two columns, so the five tiles need seven;
at a 12rem minimum 1280x720 had room for six. At 11rem it has seven, and 1366x768 keeps seven.

viewports.
