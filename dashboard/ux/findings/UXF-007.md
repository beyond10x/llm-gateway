At 1366x768 and 1280x720, in light and dark, the Routes panel is half the page wide and its table has
13 columns, so the header ends in "Cach" (errors) or "Out t" (idle) and Cache hit, In tok/s, Out
tok/s and Server run / wait lie beyond the panel edge. Nothing marks that the table scrolls: no fade,
no shadow, no scrollbar visible at rest. A person reading a rejected or slow route cannot see its
cache hit or throughput without finding the hidden scroll. Heuristic 3 (nothing required hidden).
Sessions: idle, busy, token-flow, errors. One line: give Routes the full row, or show a scroll cue
and drop low-value columns at these widths.
