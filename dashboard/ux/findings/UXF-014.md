The session and route names are the key data of the panel and are drawn at 12 px; the 14 px rule
(heuristic 15) is measured on DOM text only, so the round reports "key font" without them. An idle
string (a session that called a route within 5 minutes but has nothing in flight) is the only mark
that the pair is connected, and it is drawn at about 1.6:1 against the panel in light (about 1.8:1 in
dark), under the 3:1 of non-text contrast; in review-flow-busy-1280-light.png the two grey strings are
barely there. At 390 px the route names are cut to "claude-hai…" and "claude-son…" with no
full value anywhere in the panel (the canvas is aria-hidden; the Routes table below has them).
Sessions: busy, token-flow, errors. Cost S. One line: draw labels at 14 px or add them to the
key-font measure, and raise the idle string alpha to reach 3:1.

Closed in R-04 for contrast: flow.ts exports how each mark is drawn, FlowCanvas paints with it, and
the regression test computes each mark over `--panel` in both themes. Idle strings go from 1.55:1
to 3.54:1 in light and 1.78:1 to 4.48:1 in dark; in-flight strings from 1.99:1 to 4.24:1 and
2.49:1 to 5.54:1; nodes and dots stay above 3:1 (now 4.40:1 to 6.66:1), labels and notes above
4.5:1. Not done: the labels stay 12 px and are not in the key-font measure, and at 390 px the
route names are still cut with no full value in the panel (the Routes table has them).
