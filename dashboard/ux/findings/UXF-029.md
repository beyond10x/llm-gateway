The new column is headed "Failed, last 5 min" and reads "1 of 3" for claude-sonnet in busy, and
the next two columns, Calls and Errors, read 160 and 2 for the same route. They count since the
gateway started, which only a tooltip says (the dotted underline on the header). A reader who
takes the row left to right sees 1 failed, then 160 calls, then 2 errors, and has no word on the
screen that says why the 1 and the 2 differ or why "of 3" and "160" sit in one row. At 390 px the
header has no tooltip at all (no pointer), and the Calls column is the one cut by the scroll box
(review-routes-390-light.png). The Problems strip counts the first only, so the strip's number and
the table's agree (UXF-026 holds closed); what is missing is the window of the other two. In
errors the three agree by chance (4, 4, 4). Sessions: busy at every viewport, errors, token-flow.
Heuristic 5. Cost S. One line: put the window in the header text ("Calls, all time" or a second
line "since start") the way the failed column carries "last 5 min".
