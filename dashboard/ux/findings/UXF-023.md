fold is never pictured: the tables a monitor exists for, the expanded attempts panel and the
worst-moment entries of the layout-shift metric. The R-02 and R-03 report reviews raised it and it
is still true in R-05; UXF-019 could not be shown on a sheet for the same reason, and the R-05
review had to take extra shots by hand. Sessions: all, both themes, every viewport. Cost S. One
line: write a full-page shot per scenario and state (and one with the first call expanded) beside
the viewport sheet, and a shot at the worst layout-shift moment.
