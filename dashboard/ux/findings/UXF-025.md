After the stream fails and one poll fails, the header reads "offline" in red and the Problems strip
has one error line: "Connection: the gateway does not answer; the page shows the last snapshot".
Nothing says how old that snapshot is. Below it the Token flow keeps a thick blue "in flight"
line, its dots keep moving (two frames of the flow box 0.7 s apart differ, 3 s after the loss), and
the panel reads "1 active session". Uptime ("up 1m 34s") stops with no mark that it stopped. A
reader who looks at the flow, not the strip, reads a call that is streaming now. Heuristic 13:
the cue is wrong and one glance at the strip is the recovery, so the cost is low, but the strip
gives no "as of" time and the animation is the loudest thing on the page. Sessions: offline, both
themes, every viewport. Cost S. One line: name the snapshot's age in the line ("last snapshot 40 s
ago") and stop the flow's motion and in-flight emphasis while the connection is not live.
