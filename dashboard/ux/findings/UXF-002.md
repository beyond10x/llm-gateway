In every scenario at both gating viewports and in both themes, axe-core reports the routes table's
scroll container (and, with data, the in-flight and pods containers) as a scrollable region without
keyboard access: 1 to 2 nodes per replay, 4 at 390x844. Chromium 1254 focuses such a scroller on Tab
by itself, so the harness's own keyboard walk reaches it; browsers without keyboard-focusable
scrollers do not. Fixed when the containers carry `tabindex="0"`, a role and an accessible name (or
stop scrolling); the fix removes the `gate5-axe` entry in `e2e/known-failures.ts`.
