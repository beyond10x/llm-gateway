A person who wants the dark theme on a light system (or the reverse) cannot get it. The harness looks
for a radiogroup, combobox, switch or button named theme, dark, light or appearance and found none in
any scenario, viewport or theme. Fixed when a toggle in the header sets the theme in every replay; the
fix removes the `theme-toggle` entry in `e2e/known-failures.ts`.
