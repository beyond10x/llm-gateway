In busy, the Status tile of the first subscription limit shows the raw header value
`allowed_warning` at 1.4rem and wraps it after "allowed_warni". A state word that a person reads out
(heuristic 7) should stay whole: a label such as "allowed, warning" or a smaller value size.

Closed in R-04: a limit tile value wraps only between words or after an underscore, never inside a
word (`overflow-wrap: normal`, `hyphens: none`, a `<wbr>` after each underscore). The status
`allowed_warning` reads "allowed (warning)"; every status, overage, claim and overage reason keeps
its raw header value in its title. The regression test replays busy and errors at every state and
at every viewport the round measures and finds no word on two lines; before the fix "warning" broke
at 1366x768, 1536x754 and 960x1080, and no status tile carried a title.
