A session label is "<first 8 characters of the id>… · <client name>" (`sessionLabel`). The route
names are tested whole at every viewport (UXF-014), the session labels are not. At 390x844 the left
column keeps room for about 9 characters, so the three sessions of token-flow read "5d0c7e19… ·…",
"e83f10b6… ·…" and "1a7b2fe0… ·…" and busy reads "3f9a1c0e · …" and "8b27d4aa · …": the client
name (claude-code, codex, opencode) is gone and a bare " · …" is left, which says nothing. The only
way to tell the sessions apart is a prefix of a hash, and the Sessions table below names them by
client. At 1366 and 1280 the label is whole. Heuristics 7 and 10. Sessions: busy, token-flow,
errors, 390 only. Cost S. One line: at narrow widths drop the id and keep the client name (or draw
the name and cut the id), and test the label like the route names.
