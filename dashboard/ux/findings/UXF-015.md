At 390x844 about 20 % of the theme toggle (radiogroup "Theme") lies outside the viewport in every
scenario, both themes. The header does not wrap, so the last segment runs past the right edge.
The gate checks the header only at the gating viewports, so no gate caught it.
Fixed when the toggle is fully in view and uncovered at every viewport the round measures.

Diagnosed in R-04: at 390x844 the header wraps (`flex-wrap: wrap`) and the toggle sits alone on its
second line, inside the viewport and under nothing. The 0.2 came from one of the probe's five sample
points: the bottom-right one, 2 px inside the corner of the box, lies outside the toggle's 6 px
rounded corner (the toggle clips its segments with `overflow: hidden`), where the hit test finds the
header. At the gating viewports the same point fell on the toggle's 1 px border by subpixel position
only. The page is unchanged; the probe now moves each corner point onto the curve of a rounded
corner. Gate 2 runs at every viewport the round measures (1366x768, 1280x720, 1536x754, 960x1080,
390x844), in both themes, at every state and action, and is green; it failed at 390x844 in every
scenario before the probe fix. The probe's own break test (`e2e/tools.e2e.ts`, "break: a rounded
control fully in view and uncovered reads 1") read 0.2 before the fix.
