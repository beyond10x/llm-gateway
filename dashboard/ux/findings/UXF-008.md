The fixed canvas draws session and route labels and curves behind the opaque panels, so only slivers
show in the 16 px page gutters: a grey dot with a hairline at the right edge, one or two characters
of a label at the left edge, diagonal strokes between panels (contact-1366x768-dark.png, token-flow
and errors; review-errors-full-dark.png). They name nothing a person can read and look like rendering
glitches. Heuristic 11 (no motion or mark without cause), and heuristic 12 as the labels repeat
names the tables already carry. Sessions: busy, token-flow, errors. One line: show the layer only
where it is readable, or drop it.

Closed in R-03 (decision R-03: yes): the fixed background layer is gone. The token flow is a
full-width "Token flow" section at the top of main, above Overview; its canvas is 180 px high (140 px
below 600 px wide) and draws the session labels (first 8 characters of the id, then the client), the
route labels, the strings and the particles inside the panel in both themes. A caption in the DOM
counts the active sessions and routes and names the input and output colours; with no active session
the panel says "No active sessions.". At 1366x768 and 1280x720 the canvas lies fully in the viewport
and is the element hit at every point of the grid (flow.visible_ratio 1, from 0.005 to 0.09).

The harness gate `flow: the token-flow canvas is drawn, also under reduced motion, and at least 95 %
in view and uncovered` in `e2e/gates.e2e.ts` passes unmarked once this panel lands; its `flow` entry in
`e2e/known-failures.ts` is removed. Since R-04 it runs on every scenario with an active session, and
the ratchet holds `flow.visible_ratio` and `flow.drawn` per scenario.
